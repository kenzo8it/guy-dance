📱 Put Guy Dance on your phone

1. Add it to your home screen. Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen like an app.

2. Get rewarded automatically (iPhone). Open the Shortcuts app → Automation → + → App. Choose Instagram, tick Is Closed (untick Is Opened), and pick Run Immediately. Add the action Open URLs with:

Now every time you close Instagram, a guy joins the queue backstage. Add the same automation for TikTok, X, or any other app you want to resist.

3. Come back tomorrow. Everyone you earned today comes out and dances.
